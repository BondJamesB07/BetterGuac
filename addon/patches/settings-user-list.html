Users

  • Everyone who can sign in to BetterGuac. Click a person to edit them, or New user to add one.
  • Admin manages users and servers. User only sees the servers assigned to them and their own profile.
  • MFA on: two-factor sign-in is set up. MFA not set up: they'll be asked to scan a QR code the next time they sign in.
  • The number of servers counts everything that person can open: servers given to them one by one plus the servers in their groups. Their server groups show as tags.
  • New accounts sign in with their email address. Older accounts marked "Signs in with a username" keep signing in the way they always have.
