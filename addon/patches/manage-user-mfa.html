Two-factor sign-in

  • For a person who lost their phone or got a new one and can't sign in.
  • Reset removes their current authenticator and replaces their password with a one-time password, shown here once.
  • Give them the one-time password in person or by phone. At sign-in they choose a new password, then scan a new QR code and confirm it with a code.
  • Their old password stops working, so someone who only knows it can't set up the new code.
  • To reset your own, use My profile, which asks for your password and a current code.
